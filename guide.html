💡 Basic Tips & Getting Started

Essential tips for new players

🎮 Game Controls

Mouse/Touch: Tap and drag to aim your water stream

Strategy: Keep your finger on the screen for continuous water flow

Tip: Practice aiming while moving for better control

💰 Coin Collection

Primary Goal: Collect as many coins as possible

Strategy: Focus on coins in safe areas first

Tip: Some coins are hidden near fire sources - plan your route

🚒 Water Management

Limited Resource: Your water supply is limited

Strategy: Use short bursts instead of continuous spray

Tip: Water refills automatically over time

🎯 Advanced Strategies

Pro techniques for high scores

  • 🔥 Fire Prioritization: Always extinguish the largest fires first - they spread faster and are more dangerous
  • 💎 Coin Path Optimization: Plan your route to collect coins efficiently while putting out fires
  • ⚡ Combo System: Chain fire extinguishing and coin collection for bonus points
  • 🎯 Precision Aiming: Aim for the base of flames for maximum effectiveness
  • 🛡️ Defensive Play: Sometimes it's better to let small fires burn and focus on coin collection

📊 Level Progression Guide

What to expect as you advance

🌟 Levels 1-10: Tutorial Phase

Learn basic controls and game mechanics. Fires are small and slow-spreading. Focus on mastering water control and basic coin collection.

🔥 Levels 11-25: Challenge Begins

Fires become more aggressive and spread faster. Coin placement becomes more strategic. Start using advanced techniques.

⚡ Levels 26-50: Expert Zone

Multiple simultaneous fires, complex coin layouts, and time pressure. Requires perfect strategy execution.
